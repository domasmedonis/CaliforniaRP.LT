# CaliforniaRP.LT – Gamemode & UCP

CaliforniaRP.LT is a [RageMP](https://rage.mp/) modification for Grand Theft Auto V multiplayer, featuring a custom gamemode and a web-based User Control Panel (UCP).




Upload this into existing RageMP server files - https://wiki.rage.mp/wiki/Getting_Started_with_Server


## Gamemode Features

- **Login & Registration**  
  Character applications, admin approval, and MySQL-backed character selection.

- **Text Roleplay & Character Identity**  
  Proximity `/me` and `/do`, die-only `/roll`, editable character profiles, and close-range `/showid` with driver-license status.

- **Banking & ATM System**  
  Manage your in-game money.

- **Phone System**  
  Contacts, calls, messages, Drive app, Twitter app, mobile banking

- **Basic Roleplay Commands**  
  Local speech, whisper, shout, OOC, private messages, and non-forcing roleplay rolls.

- **Emergency Dispatch & Case Records**  
  Persistent 911 reports with accepted/en-route/on-scene/closed states, service notes, and incident links for fines, warrants, arrests, and medical treatment.

- **Police MDC & Medical Records**  
  On-duty PD can search people, vehicles, fines, warrants, and arrest history; open warrants can be served through a cuffed arrest, and jail sentences persist through disconnects. On-duty MD can view patient treatment history linked to dispatch cases.

- **Player-Run Drive Trips**  
  Ride requests, passenger pickup, shared-vehicle start, mutually accepted fares, cash settlement, receipts, and recent trip history.

- **Admin System**  
  Minimal functionality for basic server management.

- **Vehicle dealership**
  Buy vehicle at Los Santos dealership

- **Inventory**
  Inventory system with I button or /inv

- **Barber and clothes shop**
  You can modify your clothes and beard, hair.

- **Housing and business systems**
  Add a house or a business

- **Player-Run Casinos & Betting Houses**  
  Admins can create `casino` or `bettinghouse` venues with `/abiz create` and assign player owners with `/abiz setowner`. Casinos offer server-settled roulette with a funded house reserve. Betting-house owners post A/B events; when betting closes, the server randomly locks the result. Owners can only settle that stored result, while open events can be voided for refunds. Wagers and payouts are logged.

- **Pawn-Shop Consignment**  
  Sell pawnable items at a nearby pawn shop for its buyback rate. The owner reviews pending offers, collects and renames items, sets a resale price, then lists them for customers or gives an unlisted item to a nearby player.

- **House Drug Production**  
  Plant up to 10 admin-granted cannabis seeds per owned house; harvest 30 grams per plant after 72 real-time hours. Admins can enable a house lab with `/allowhdrugs [propertyID]`; abstract, drug-specific reagent sets synthesize in five minutes. Use `/hdrugs` at your house to check status, plant, harvest, synthesize, or collect.
